About Me

Hello! I'm Pouyan Karimi, a computational engineer and scientific software developer with a multidisciplinary background spanning engineering mechanics, electrical engineering, numerical simulation, and high-performance computing.

I earned my Ph.D. in Engineering Mechanics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, with a minor in Electrical and Computer Engineering. My doctoral research, advised by Prof. Martin Ostoja-Starzewski, focused on large-scale computational modeling of complex materials and physical systems using high-performance computing, stochastic methods, numerical algorithms, and nonlinear finite element analysis. I developed scalable simulation tools for multiphysics and engineering applications, including collaborative research with Boeing on industry-scale systems.

I also hold an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and an M.S. in Structural Engineering from the University of Tehran. This interdisciplinary training has given me a strong foundation in numerical methods, computational mechanics, electromagnetics, signal processing, finite element analysis, and mathematical modeling.

My software experience includes Python and C++, scientific computing, numerical algorithm development, data analysis, and parallel computing using MPI and OpenMP. I also work with Git-based development workflows, testing and validation, automation, and the integration of data-driven and physics-based computational methods.

My current interests are centered on scientific software engineering, computational physics, simulation, high-performance computing, machine learning, and AI-assisted engineering. I am particularly interested in developing reliable and scalable software that transforms mathematical and physical models into practical tools for solving complex engineering problems.
